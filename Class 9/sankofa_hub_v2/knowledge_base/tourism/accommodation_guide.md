# Ghana Accommodation Guide

## Accra

### Luxury (USD 200–500/night)
Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast City — 5-star, located in Accra's Airport City district. Business-oriented, conference facilities, multiple restaurants, spa. Popular with international business travellers and diplomats.

Labadi Beach Hotel — beachfront 5-star property, approximately 10km from central Accra. Large pool, private beach access, multiple restaurants. One of Accra's most recognisable landmark hotels.

Alisa Hotel North Ridge — established 5-star with large event and conference facilities. Consistently popular for corporate events.

### Mid-Range (USD 80–180/night)
Mövenpick Ambassador Hotel Accra — reliable international chain standard, central location near the airport. Business-friendly.

La Palm Royal Beach Hotel — beachfront location, slightly dated but popular with leisure travellers.

City Lodge — cleaner, functional business option in a quieter neighbourhood.

Serviced apartments in East Legon and Cantonments offer good value for longer stays. Check Airbnb and booking platforms for options from USD 60/night upward.

### Budget (USD 20–50/night)
Accra has a growing range of clean guesthouses and hostels, particularly in Osu and East Legon. The Drop Inn Hostel is a well-known backpacker option with dormitory and private room options. Airbnb presence is significant in Accra, offering apartments from USD 30/night. Always read recent reviews carefully as quality varies.

## Cape Coast & Surrounds

Coconut Grove Beach Resort — beachfront mid-range property, popular with international visitors. Restaurant with good local seafood.

Savoy Hotel Cape Coast — reliable city-centre mid-range option, good base for visiting the castle and Kakum.

Hans Cottage Botel — unique property adjacent to Kakum National Park with resident crocodiles in the grounds. A memorable stay for nature visitors.

Budget guesthouses in Cape Coast town from GHS 100–250/night. Ask at the tourist information office near the castle for current recommendations.

## Kumasi

Golden Tulip Kumasi City — the main international-standard hotel in Ghana's second city. Good restaurant, conference facilities.

Royal Basin Hotel — popular mid-range option, well-located near the Kejetia market area.

Many guesthouses throughout the city cater to the large domestic business traveller base at reasonable prices.

## Northern Ghana (Mole / Tamale)

Mole Motel — the only accommodation inside Mole National Park. Essential to book well in advance during peak season (November–March). Basic rooms and self-contained cottages; the viewing platform overlooking a watering hole is the highlight of the stay. Elephant sightings from the terrace are common in the dry season.

Zaina Lodge — premium eco-lodge near Mole, fully solar-powered. More comfortable and expensive than Mole Motel. Guided safari walks included.

Tamale city has several mid-range hotels suitable for stopovers en route to Mole: Picorna Hotel and the Mariam Hotel are reliable options.

## Volta Region (Hohoe / Wli / Afadjato)

The Volta Region is less developed for tourism accommodation but growing fast. Guesthouses in Hohoe town serve as the base for visiting Wli Waterfalls. Mountain Paradise Hotel near Afadjato is a popular budget option. Book ahead during peak weekends as capacity is limited.

## General Booking Advice
- Book in advance during peak season (December–January, Easter weekend, August Diaspora season).
- Check whether breakfast is included — many Ghanaian hotels include it in the room rate.
- Negotiating room rates is acceptable at independently owned properties, especially for multi-night stays.
- International booking platforms (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.ng) work well for Accra's larger hotels. For smaller properties, direct phone booking is often more reliable.
- Confirm your booking 24–48 hours before arrival — cancellations and overbookings do occur.
