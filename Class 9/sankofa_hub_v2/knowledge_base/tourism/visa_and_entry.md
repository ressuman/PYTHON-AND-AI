# Ghana Visa & Entry Requirements

## Overview
Ghana welcomes visitors from around the world. Visa requirements depend on your nationality. As of 2025, holders of ECOWAS passports (all 15 West African member states) do not require a visa and may enter freely. African Union member states have varying bilateral arrangements.

## Who Needs a Visa
Most non-African nationals require a visa to enter Ghana. This includes citizens of the United States, United Kingdom, European Union member states, Canada, Australia, China, and India. Always verify current requirements through the Ghana Immigration Service website (gis.gov.gh) or the nearest Ghana embassy, as policies change.

## Visa on Arrival
Ghana does not currently offer a general visa-on-arrival scheme for most nationalities. A limited e-Visa programme has been operational. Check gis.gov.gh for current status and eligible nationalities.

## E-Visa Application Process
Applications through the Ghana Immigration Service portal require: valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond intended stay), return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, yellow fever vaccination certificate, passport-sized photograph, and the visa fee (USD 60–100 depending on visa type and nationality — verify current fees as these change). Processing time: typically 3–5 business days for standard processing.

## Yellow Fever Certificate
A valid yellow fever vaccination certificate is MANDATORY for all travellers entering Ghana. This is enforced at the border. No certificate = no entry. Ensure vaccination is administered at least 10 days before travel.

## Airport of Entry
Kotoka International Airport (KIA) in Accra is the main international arrival point. The Kumasi Airport handles some regional flights. Takoradi Airport has limited scheduled services.

## Immigration on Arrival
Immigration queues at KIA can be long during peak hours. Automated immigration kiosks (SmartGate) are available for eligible passport holders. Declare any currency above USD 10,000.

## Length of Stay
Tourist visas are typically valid for 30–60 days with single or multiple entry. Extensions can be applied for at the Ghana Immigration Service offices in Accra. Overstaying your visa results in fines and potential deportation.

## Important Notes for Visitors
- Keep your passport and visa documentation on your person or in a secure location at all times.
- Register with your country's embassy on arrival for extended stays.
- Verify all visa fees and requirements directly with the Ghana High Commission or Embassy in your country — requirements and fees change and this document may not reflect the most current information.
